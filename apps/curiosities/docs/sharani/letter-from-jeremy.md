I want to show you the main screen of Curiomatic and ask for your help making it better.

**What I modeled it on.** I tried to make this window feel like Final Cut Pro. Across the top are viewer windows. One plays a film from our curated list, as a quick animated storyboard for now. Next to it is a viewer for the film we're making. You can add as many inspiration films as you like. On the right there's an inspector, like Final Cut Pro's, with controls grouped by the big filmmaking categories: camera, performance, light and color, set, wardrobe, sound, feeling, comedy, effects, story, editing, and page layout. The fine details fold away inside each one so it doesn't get overwhelming. Underneath is a timeline, like a music app or Final Cut's tracks, where every curiosity is its own track running left to right.

**Update: CapCut is now the main model.** I've decided CapCut's interface is even better than Final Cut Pro's, so the screen now follows CapCut's layout and dark look. The categories run as a row of icon tabs at the top left, next to a sidebar of groups and a grid of cards you can drop onto the timeline. The player sits in the middle, the details panel is on the right, and the curiosity tracks run along the bottom timeline. Every editing feature in CapCut and Final Cut Pro (transitions, filters, adjustments, text, speed, the audio mix, masks) is now a curiosity too.

You can take a curiosity from one inspiration film, or the same curiosity from a few of them, and blend it into your own film.

**What I'd love from you.** Final Cut Pro is what I know. You know 3D animation programs far better than I do, so please make the windows and the interface feel like the ones you love most. Use Maya, Blender, Houdini, or anything else that feels right to you. The best way to do it is to drag the user manual for that program into Claude. Then guide Claude on how the four building blocks should live inside that kind of interface:

- **Curiosities**: one thing to look at, like shot size or how warm the light is
- **Curiosity suites**: a group of curiosities that belong together
- **Curiosity proximities**: when one thing happens, another follows
- **Curiosity proximity suites**: a group of those cause-and-effect pairs working together

Bring along your favorite everyday features from those programs too. A lot of them can be thought of as curiosities, and they should be. If it's a setting a filmmaker changes, it can be a curiosity with its own track.

**A reminder about proximities.** On the timeline, each curiosity track has nodes, which are points where you set a value. You make a proximity by dragging a node from one track onto a node on another track, and a line connects them. From then on those two nodes travel together. If you like a proximity, or a whole proximity suite, you can copy it and paste it into a different scene, and every connected node and line comes with it.
