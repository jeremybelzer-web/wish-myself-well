## The database

3. **Built as plain script files that set `window.CuriosityDB`**, the same no-build style as the rest of the app, plus a JSON copy for other tools. Reason: the app is static files with no build step; a Maya panel or desktop app can read the JSON.
4. **A curiosity is a lens with sliders.** Every slider is graded: a scale of words in order or a number range, with a default from, to and curve. Every item at every level also gets an Amount slider, and the on/off trigger is the item itself. Reason: your 12:09Z and 12:36Z notes (A/B is just on/off; each item has its own graded, automatable parts).
5. **Suites get a Blend slider and a weight per member; proximities get Cause, Delay, How often and Effect size; proximity suites get a delay and how often per member.** Reason: matches what automation.js already does, so nothing in the app has to change shape.
6. **Every existing catalog row was brought in by a script (`tools/import-catalog.js`)**, not retyped: 170 curiosities, 49 suites, 15 proximities, 5 proximity suites. 14 rows that were free text (eyelines, touch, palette...) were given simple scales so they can be automated. Reason: one source of truth, re-runnable if the catalog changes.
7. **Every catalog row now has at least three sliders of its own** (its setting plus two more, such as "how noticeable" or "how it changes": holds, drifts, steps or snaps). Reason: "all of them need to be automatable" with their own sub-parameters.
8. **Music is three curiosities: Music, No music, and Sound around the scene.** Music has 18 sliders (how present, which track from your own list, who hears it, tempo, mood, matches or fights the scene, famous or original, lyrics, cuts on the beat, how it comes in and leaves...). No music has its own sliders (how long, what fills the gap, how the music vanishes, what the gap is for). Reason: your example, music or the lack of it; making silence its own curiosity lets it be automated on its own.
9. **"Which track" is a list you fill with your own tracks**, not real song names. Reason: copyright; the database names devices, not songs.
10. **Emotion builds on the app's Emotion and Emotional road lenses**: adds unhappy to happy and calm to wound up to the feeling itself; height, swings, turning points, lowest and highest point and where it ends to each character's road; tension, laughter, release, light-and-dark alternation and where the peak falls to the film's road; plus the feeling gap between two characters. Reason: your 12:36Z emotion answer.
11. **Comedy now spans 25 curiosities in the Comedy workspace and 6 in Comedy from the mix**, with 14 suites, 14 proximities and 5 proximity suites. Added on top of the app's comedy lenses: light to dark, mean or kind, pace of the exchange, hold after the laugh, escalation steps and ceiling, how obvious the setup is, reactions to camera, status flips and falls, straight one and funny one; plus running gags, callbacks, rule of three, slapstick, wordplay, misdirection, irony, understatement, fish out of water, visual gags, subversion, comic editing and comic sound. Reason: "Comedy really needs to be central".
12. **Wardrobe and set design build on the app's lenses** (see 20): the main character's clothes gain loudness, layers, accessories, hair and makeup, "true to them" (own style to disguise) and change since the last scene; background clothes gain formality and color coding; set design gains style family, ceiling height, wealth, symmetry, windows, main color, personal or anonymous, real or stylized. Props are a new curiosity. Reason: your bar example, without listing anything twice.
13. **Color builds on the app's Color lens** (adds main hue, kind of look such as sepia, overall brightness, film or digital look). **Lighting is a new lens that gathers the existing light rows as its sliders** and adds five (where the light seems to come from, light on the face, moving light, colored light, shadows). Reason: the light rows already existed; a lens that points at them avoids two copies.
14. **Three new workspaces are proposed**: Music & sound, Editing & structure, Page & panel, marked `proposed`; the app thread decides whether they become tabs. Reason: music and the catalog's Sound, Story, Edit and Comic rows had no workspace.
15. **The character matrix (PR #6) is in the database** with the same ids the matrix registers (`cm-stability`...), so both drive the same automation. Each of the nine type patterns is a suite and each stress and growth arrow a proximity. The patterns are marked as estimates to check. Reason: coordinator note at 12:39Z.
16. **I don't edit the app thread's files.** The PR description and `data/README.md` give the exact lines to add to `index.html` (load the files, then `CuriosityDB.install({ CURIOSITIES, SUITES, PROXIMITIES })`, and after automation.js `CuriosityDB.installAutomation(CurioAuto)`). Reason: the brief says the "Bring Maya into the app" thread owns the app code.
17. **Tested by loading a throwaway copy of index.html with those lines in headless Chromium**: no page errors, 1,093 automation parameters, and the Music module opens with its 17 lanes. The copy was deleted. Reason: prove the instructions work before handing them over.

## VCV Rack bridge (15:30Z, coordinator's ask, roadmap Phase 6)
