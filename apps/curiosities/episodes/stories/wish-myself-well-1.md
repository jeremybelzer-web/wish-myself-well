# Wish Myself Well · Season 1, Episode 1 · "Crazy Loser (kidding)"

This is the story file for the storyboard. Edit it any way you like, or type new story pieces in the thread and Claude will put them in.

## How to read and edit this file

- **Every line that starts with "- " is one panel, and one panel is one second of film.**
- To make a picture stay up longer, put the seconds in brackets at the end, like `(3s)`. That line becomes 3 panels of the same picture.
- To add a moment, add a new "- " line where it happens. To cut one, delete the line. Nothing needs renumbering.
- The first word says how much we see:
  - **WIDE**: the whole place, people small.
  - **MEDIUM**: a person from about the waist up.
  - **CLOSE**: one face.
  - **TINY**: one small thing filling the picture (a hand, a napkin, a phone).
  - **BLACK**: a black screen with words on it.
- After the first word, a few optional camera words, split by commas:
  - **low** (camera looks up at them), **high** (camera looks down), **overhead** (straight down), **floor** (camera on the floor)
  - **push in** (camera slowly moves closer), **pull out** (moves back), **circle** (goes around them), **follow** (moves along with them), **pan** (turns its head), **tilted** (picture leans)
  - **still** (camera on a stand, not moving at all), **shaky** (held in the hand)
  - **through the Lens** (we see what the Lens glasses see)
  - **setup** and **payoff** mark where a joke is planted and where it lands
- A feeling in square brackets, like `[anxious 3]`, changes the feeling from that panel on. The number is how strong, 0 to 5. Feelings the app knows: loving, joyful, curious, melancholy, anxious, fearful, angry, triumphant, absurd, dreamlike.
- Spoken words go after a dash: `— Biju: "Wipe your face."` About two or three words fit in one second, so longer lines get `(2s)` or are split over two panels.
- Each scene starts with a few plain settings: where, who, the light (dusk, flat, lamp, hard sun, moon), the air (still, wind, crowd, rain, moving), the color (warm, neutral, cool), and the kind of comedy.

## Scene 1. The napkin

Where: A rain city at dusk, in and around Biju's tuk-tuk
Who: Biju, Passenger
Light: dusk
Air: rain
Color: warm
Feeling: curious 1

- MEDIUM. Biju, in the driver's seat, eats a slice of mango. Calm.
- MEDIUM. A passenger climbs into the back, soaked, staring at a phone.
- TINY. The phone: a number counting down fast, in red. [anxious 3]
- CLOSE. The passenger's face, lit red. — Passenger: "No. No no."
- TINY. The number hits zero.
- CLOSE. — Passenger: "It's gone." [anxious 4]
- CLOSE, push in. — Passenger: "I had a year in there." (2s)
- MEDIUM. Biju looks in the little mirror. Says nothing.
- CLOSE. Biju's eyes in the mirror. Kind. Not worried at all.
- MEDIUM. — Passenger: "Say something." [angry 3]
- MEDIUM, still. Biju keeps looking. Rain drums on the roof. (2s)
- TINY. Biju's hand opens the glove box.
- TINY. Inside: a neat stack of paper napkins.
- TINY. He takes one napkin. The raindrops start to stop in the air. [dreamlike 3]
- WIDE, circle. The camera goes all the way around the tuk-tuk. Every raindrop hangs, frozen. (3s)
- CLOSE, low. Biju turns in his seat and holds the napkin out with both hands, careful, like the last wire on a bomb.
- TINY. The napkin between two thumbs. Drops hang all around it. (2s)
- CLOSE. The passenger stares at it.
- CLOSE. — Biju: "Wipe your face."

- TINY, payoff. The fingers take it. The rain falls again. [loving 3]
- MEDIUM. The passenger wipes their face. Cries a little. Laughs a little.
- MEDIUM. Biju turns back and starts the engine. — Biju: "Where to?"
- CLOSE. — Passenger: "...I don't know."
- MEDIUM. Biju nods, delighted. — Biju: "Good. Me neither." [joyful 3]
- WIDE, high, pull out. The tuk-tuk putters off into the rain. (2s)
- WIDE. A cat watches from a dry doorway. [absurd 1]
