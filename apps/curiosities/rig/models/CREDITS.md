# Free 3D characters

These three rigged characters come from the Khronos Group's glTF sample models
(https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets). They were downloaded unchanged on 2026-10-03, and only
the file names were changed. The app shows each credit under the character.

| cesium-man.glb | Cesium Man (19 joints, one walk, textured) | © 2017 Cesium. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The Cesium logo on the shirt is Cesium's trademark and is not covered by the license. |
