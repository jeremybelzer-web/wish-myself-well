# Reference from the music app

Text decoded from two of Jeremy's Pages documents for his music app, where curiosities are called essences. They show how the music app extracts, applies, expands and automates each essence. Read them for the pattern, not the code: line numbers point into the music app's editor, which is not in this repository.

- `music-app-essence-inventory.txt`: what the music app ships today, and the six-column test.
- `music-app-essence-candidates.txt`: essences the music app may still be missing.
