=ESSENCE CANDIDATES — musical variables the record may have missed
Written:August 13, 2026, THIRTY-SECOND session (Fable), at the client's direct request:"I'd like help thinking of any essence / music variable that I might have missed."Status: a BRAINSTORM awaiting the client's read-through— brain-drain protocol: nothing here entersROADMAP.mduntil he picks. The test every candidate is held to is his, verbatim (August 13):"does this make an essence extractable, appliable, expandable, and automate-able?"
The organizing principle this sweep surfaced:the app can already RENDER most of these (velocity, durations, tempo, cadences, melisma, space) — what is missing in each case is the EXTRACTOR: the move that lifts the variable out of a moment as a Shelf-compatible strand so it can travel to another song.Anything the app can render, it should eventually be able to extract.
0  WHAT ALREADY EXISTS (judge candidates against this, not against zero)
Shelf strands shipped:contourdnarhythmchords(validShelf, editor ~2078). The GEMS  essence list covers: Modulations  Psych-Outs  Melody contour/rhythm/Form  Song Form  Melodic Segment  Rhythmic Form + Segment  Melody Notes  notes-to-chord-tones map  Transpose Re-Shaper  tuplet reinterpretation  Rhythm of the Polyphonic Instrument  Rhythmic Modulation  Chord Changes  Arrangement  the universal variation ring  Wilder Phrasing  Instrumentation + the five instrument maps  BLANK (user-created). The audio/timing round adds feel/timing and tempo behaviour. Candidates below are what none of those quite names.
1  TIME & RHYTHM DOMAIN
HARMONIC RHYTHM— the RATE of chord change, independent of which chords. A one-chord vamp vs. two-per-bar changes is a copyable identity. Extract: chord-duration histogram from the lane. Apply: re-time another song's changes to the donor's rate. Expand: "this harmonic rhythm is to 4/4 as ___ is to 3/4." Automate: a harmonic-rhythm lane (density over time).Probably the single biggest miss — it is a classic analysis variable and nothing in the record names it.
SYNCOPATION PROFILE— where events land relative to the grid: offbeat ratio, anticipations, push/pull bias. Distinct from the rhythm pattern itself — it is the placementbias. Extract from any track's onsets vs. grid. Half-exists: the feel/timing strand (audio/timing round) carries microtiming; this is the notated-level cousin.
PHRASE-LENGTH SCHEME— 4+4 vs. 3+3+2, extensions, elisions. Wilder Phrasing names "over-bar-lines" as an operation; themapof a song's phrase lengths as an extractable object is new.
SILENCE / SPACE PROFILE— where the music breathes: rest placement and length per track.Add Spaceis the variationoperation; the extracted spacesignatureof a donor song is the essence.
ANACRUSIS BEHAVIOUR— how phrases begin: on the beat, pushed, picked up. Small, cheap to extract, very audible when transplanted.
2  PITCH & HARMONY DOMAIN
VOICE LEADING / VOICING STYLE— how chord tones CONNECT (common-tone smoothness vs. leaps) and how chords are SPACED (close/open/drop-2). Two songs with identical changes and different voicing styles sound like different bands. Extract from the comping voice. Thevoice()machinery renders this today; nothing extracts it.
TENSION CURVE— dissonance/expectation over a phrase: suspension placement, dominant prolongation, where resolution lands. Expand-hook is strong: "raise the tension the way the donor's bridge does."
PEDAL / DRONE BEHAVIOUR— a static element under changing harmony, or its inverse (ostinato riding key changes — which Orbits journeys already create by accident). Raga drone practice is adjacent and already in the app's vocabulary.
BASS-LINE GRAMMAR— root-hugging vs. inversion (the bass playing the 3rd, 5th, or 7th of a chord) vs. walking vs. pedal vs. counter-melodic. The Bass Map (GEMS ) is theguide surface; the extractable grammar of a donor bass line is the essence behind it.
REGISTER / TESSITURA PATH— where material sits in pitch space and how that moves section-to-section (verse low, chorus up an octave). Trivially extractable; hugely audible; automate-able as a register lane.
3  SOUND & PERFORMANCE DOMAIN
DYNAMICS / ENERGY ARC— velocity contour within a phrase; energy map across a song (build, drop, plateau).n[8]velocity ships and is read by the MIDI writer; no extractor exists. Automate-hook is the strongest of any candidate (it IS an automation curve).
ARTICULATION / GATE PROFILE— sounded length vs. slot length: staccato funk vs. legato pad. Extract: duration/gap ratios per track. Cheap, and instantly transplantable.
TEXTURE / DENSITY CURVE— voices sounding at once, over time: thin verse, thick chorus. Extract from the grid across tracks. Pairs with Arrangement (GEMS ) but is its continuous, automate-able shadow.
HUMANIZATION PROFILE— micro-timing + micro-velocity variance as a joint fingerprint (drummer's feel beyond the groove pattern). Extends the audio/timing feel strand with the velocity dimension.
ORNAMENTATION DENSITY & PLACEMENT— where melisma/gamaka/grace-note events go and how often. The melisma machinery ships (n[7].pb, the rules pack); thedensity mapof a donor performance as a copyable essence is new.
4  STRUCTURE & RELATIONSHIP DOMAIN
TRANSITION CRAFT— how section boundaries are handled: fill, riser, dropout, the bar of silence before a chorus. A moment-scale essence (it lives AT the boundary), which makes it a natural Orbits/travel companion.
CALL-AND-RESPONSE / INTERPLAY— the relationship BETWEEN two tracks: melody answers bass, gap-filling, question/answer phrasing. The first inherently two-track essence; the This-is-to-That engine's natural home ("melody:bass in the donor :: melody:? here").
REPETITION SCHEME— what repeats, how many times, before variation arrives (loop-vs-develop ratio at motif scale). The variation ring supplies the operations; the donor'sscheduleof when to vary is the essence.
HOOK PLACEMENT— where the memorable cell recurs and at what interval (title line position, post-chorus echo). Corpus-friendly (the McGill Billboard set could ground it).
CONTRAST MAP— the A:B relationships a song maintains (verse:chorus :: sparse:dense :: low:high). This is the meta-essence the This-is-to-That tool needs as input — arguably the CYOA layer's native data structure.
5  IF VOCALS ARE IN SCOPE (client call — the app is instrumental-first today)
LYRIC PROSODY— syllable rhythm against the melody; where stressed syllables land. 22.RHYME SCHEME / DENSITY.23.VOWEL COLOR PATH— open/closed vowel placement across a phrase (adjacent to the melisma engine's concerns).
6  THE SESSION'S TOP PICKS (labelled as the session's, not the client's)
By the four-fold test with existing substrate: Harmonic rhythm(biggest named gap, pure extraction from data that ships)  Dynamics/energy arc(velocity already inn[8]; strongest automate story)  Articulation/gate profile(cheapest extractor in the list)  Voice leading/voicing style(largest audible payoff)  Contrast map(the CYOA/This-is-to-That enabler — a meta-essence the guided layer will need regardless).
