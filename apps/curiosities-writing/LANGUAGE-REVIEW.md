# Language curiosities: review list

102 writing-only curiosities (generated by tools/review-list.js). Every row is marked **keep**; change it to **drop** or **change** and say what.

Every countable one (marked ★) also has: how often, where in the sentence, spacing, gap between two, what it is about. Every row also has: narration or dialogue, spread across characters, which narrative voice, change from sentence to sentence.
